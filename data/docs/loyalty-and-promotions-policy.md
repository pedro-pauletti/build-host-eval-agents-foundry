# Loyalty and Promotions Policy
This policy explains ZavaCore membership, member pricing, promotion stacking, clearance rules, and final-sale treatment.

## ZavaCore membership
ZavaCore membership is Zava's customer loyalty program for B2C shoppers. Members may receive early access to product drops, member-only pricing, birthday offers, free standard shipping thresholds, and exchange conveniences. Membership benefits apply to eligible ZavaCore Field products in Core, Pro, Premium, and Elite lines.

Membership does not guarantee inventory availability. High-demand sizes and colorways may sell out quickly, especially in Premium and Elite launch windows. Inventory status remains governed by available stock at the assigned distribution center or retail store.

## Member pricing and eligibility
Member pricing may be offered by product line, garment, size, colorway, channel, or campaign. A member price is valid only during the published promotion period and only for eligible customers signed into a Zava account or identified at a flagship store. Store associates may not manually apply member pricing to ineligible transactions.

Pro-style team or group offers, when available, are separate from individual ZavaCore membership. If two eligible prices are visible for the same item, Zava applies the single best eligible price unless the offer terms explicitly permit combination.

## Promotion stacking rules
Only one product discount may apply to a line item unless the promotion says stacking is allowed. A cart-level discount may combine with a product sale only when both offers allow it. Free shipping offers may combine with product discounts unless the shipment is excluded by destination, service level, or carrier limitation.

Coupons cannot be applied to previous purchases after the promotion window closes. Price adjustments for new markdowns may be considered within 7 days of purchase when the exact SKU, size, and colorway remain available and the offer is not final sale, clearance, or member-exclusive.

## Clearance and final sale
Clearance is used for discontinued colorways, end-of-season inventory, aged sizes, packaging changes, or overstock by distribution center or store. Clearance pricing may vary by channel and location. Items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged for fit, preference, or buyer's remorse.

Final-sale hygiene-sensitive items cannot be returned once opened or tried on. Defective or incorrectly shipped clearance items remain eligible for customer-care review. Customer support should clearly distinguish between a final-sale preference return, which is not allowed, and a verified defect, which may qualify for refund, replacement, or store credit.

## Promotion governance
Merchandising owns promotion strategy, Pricing publishes offer rules, and Customer Care applies customer-facing policy. Any exception that changes price, returnability, or exchange eligibility must be documented on the order. Promotions should use clear start and end dates, eligible SKU lists, channel scope, and whether the offer affects Zava Express, Swift Post, or Metro Freight shipping charges.
