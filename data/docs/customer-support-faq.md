# Customer Support FAQ
This FAQ provides consistent customer answers for ZavaCore Field order tracking, delays, exceptions, returns, exchanges, and store pickup.

## Tracking orders
**Q: How do I track my order?**  
A: Use the numeric order ID from your confirmation, such as `23561`, in your Zava account or with customer support. Zava statuses are `processing`, `in_transit`, `out_for_delivery`, and `delivered`. If the order is still `processing`, the distribution center is preparing it. If it is `out_for_delivery`, it is on the final delivery vehicle and expected that day.

**Q: Which carriers does Zava use?**  
A: Zava uses Zava Express, Swift Post, and Metro Freight. Most customer apparel orders ship by Zava Express or Swift Post. Metro Freight is typically used for large consolidated shipments or retail replenishment.

## Delays
**Q: My order is delayed by weather. Do I need to do anything?**  
A: Usually no. A Weather delay means carrier movement is slowed by unsafe conditions, storms, or network disruption. For order `23518`, the shipment is held at Zava Memphis Distribution Center due to severe winter weather and no customer action is required unless Zava contacts you.

**Q: My international order is delayed by customs. What happens next?**  
A: Customs delays can involve inspection, duty review, import documents, or recipient verification. For order `23544`, Zava Global support may contact the customer if import information is required. Zava prepares shipment documents from the order record and will explain any customer action clearly.

## Delivery exceptions
**Q: My order says Exception - Address. What should I do?**  
A: Contact Zava Customer Care with your order ID, correct street address, unit number, postal code, phone number, delivery access notes, and recipient name. For order `23575`, delivery cannot continue until the address is confirmed. Address exceptions should be resolved within 5 business days before the shipment may be returned to a distribution center.

**Q: What if the package is damaged?**  
A: A Damaged exception means the carrier or Zava identified damage that may affect delivery. Customer Care will review replacement, refund, or inspection options.

## Returns and exchanges
**Q: What is the return window?**  
A: Most ZavaCore Field apparel may be returned within 60 days of delivery, pickup, or in-store purchase if it is unworn, unwashed, and has original tags attached.

**Q: Can I return worn or opened apparel?**  
A: Opening the package is acceptable if the item remains unworn, unwashed, and tags-attached. Worn apparel is not returnable for buyer's remorse. Hygiene-sensitive final-sale items cannot be returned once opened or tried on. Defects on worn or opened apparel are reviewed case by case.

**Q: Are size exchanges free?**  
A: Yes. Eligible unworn, unwashed, tags-attached items qualify for free size exchanges within 60 days when replacement inventory is available.

## Store pickup
**Q: Where can I pick up a store order?**  
A: Zava supports pickup at `ST-AUS` Zava Austin, `ST-MIA` Zava Miami, and `ST-CHI` Zava Chicago. Bring the pickup notice and a matching ID. If you need another person to pick up the order, contact support before arrival.
