# Product Line Overview
This guide explains the ZavaCore Field Core, Pro, Premium, and Elite tiers and how to read Zava SKU codes.

## Collection structure
ZavaCore Field is Zava's B2C athletic apparel collection. The catalog is built from four product lines, three garment families, three customer groups, four sizes, and four colorways. Product lines are Core, Pro, Premium, and Elite. Garments are Tops or Tees, Shorts, and Pants. Customer groups are Mens, Womens, and Youth. Sizes are S, M, L, and XL. Colorways are Black/Orange, Charcoal/Silver, Deep Red/Red, and Teal/Orange.

All tiers are designed for field training, team travel, and everyday athletic wear, but each line has a different material package and price position.

## Core line
ZavaCore Field Core is the opening price tier. Core products use durable, easy-care performance knit or woven fabric with a comfortable everyday fit. Core is recommended for school programs, practice kits, first-time Zava customers, and customers who want reliable basics at a lower price point.

Core Tops, Shorts, and Pants prioritize durability, consistent sizing, and simple care. They are usually the best recommendation when a customer wants value and broad availability.

## Pro line
ZavaCore Field Pro is the mid-performance tier. Pro products use lighter fabrics, improved stretch, and more structured patterning than Core. Pro is intended for regular training, recreational leagues, and customers who want better movement without moving to premium pricing.

Pro is often positioned as the best balance of performance and cost. It supports both online demand and retail-store replenishment for high-velocity sizes.

## Premium line
ZavaCore Field Premium focuses on comfort, softness, and elevated finishes while maintaining training performance. Premium items are appropriate for team travel, warmups, frequent wear, and customers who want a refined look. The hero SKUs `ZCPTM-SS-S-B0` and `ZCPTM-LS-L-RR` are Premium Mens Tops.

Premium generally carries higher service-level expectations than Core and Pro because customers often buy it for specific fit, feel, and colorway preferences.

## Elite line
ZavaCore Field Elite is the highest performance and highest price tier. Elite uses the most technical fabric package, with lighter feel, improved stretch recovery, and more precise fit. Elite labels its upper-body garment as a Tee rather than a Top. It is intended for competitive athletes, launch moments, and customers seeking the most advanced ZavaCore Field product.

## SKU naming scheme
Zava SKUs follow `ZC{Line}{Garment}{Gender}-{Cut}-{Size}-{Color}`. The line code is C for Core, R for Pro, P for Premium, and E for Elite. Garment code is T for Top or Tee, S for Shorts, and P for Pants. Gender code is M, W, or Y.

Cut codes describe garment construction: SS is short sleeve, LS is long sleeve, AS is athletic shorts, and AP is athletic pants. Size is S, M, L, or XL. Color codes are B0 for Black/Orange, CS for Charcoal/Silver, RR for Deep Red/Red, and T0 for Teal/Orange. For example, `ZCPTM-LS-L-RR` means Premium Mens Long-Sleeve Top, Large, Deep Red/Red.
