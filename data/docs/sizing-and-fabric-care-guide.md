# Sizing and Fabric Care Guide
This guide helps customers choose ZavaCore Field apparel sizes and care for performance fabrics correctly.

## Fit principles
ZavaCore Field apparel is designed for athletic movement. Core generally has an easy training fit, Pro has a more structured performance fit, Premium balances softness and shape retention, and Elite is the most technical fit. Tops and Tees should allow shoulder movement without pulling across the chest. Shorts should stay secure during sprinting and lateral movement. Pants should allow bend, stride, and recovery without bagging at the knee.

When between sizes, customers who prefer compression-like performance may size down, while customers who prefer relaxed training comfort may size up. Youth sizing is intended for growing athletes and should account for movement and layering.

## Performance-fabric care
Wash ZavaCore Field apparel before first wear if preferred. Machine wash cold with similar colors. Use mild detergent and avoid bleach, fabric softener, and scent beads because additives can coat performance fibers and reduce moisture management. Turn garments inside out to protect colorways such as Black/Orange, Charcoal/Silver, Deep Red/Red, and Teal/Orange.

Close zippers, empty pockets, and wash delicate Elite pieces in a mesh bag when available. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron printed graphics, heat-transfer details, or reflective trim. Do not dry clean. High heat can damage stretch recovery and shorten garment life.

## Stains, odor, and storage
Rinse sweat-heavy garments promptly and allow them to dry before placing them in a hamper. For odor, soak in cool water with mild detergent before washing. Do not store damp apparel in a gym bag. Long-term storage should be clean, dry, and away from direct sunlight to protect fabric and color.
