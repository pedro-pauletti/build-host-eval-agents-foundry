# Returns and Exchanges Policy
This policy explains Zava's 60-day return window, apparel condition rules, worn-item handling, exchanges, and refund timelines.

## Standard return window
Zava accepts most ZavaCore Field apparel returns within 60 days of delivery, store pickup, or in-store purchase. Eligible items must be unworn, unwashed, free of odor or stains, and returned with original tags attached. Proof of purchase is required through an order number, receipt, account history, or store transaction lookup.

The policy applies to Core, Pro, Premium, and Elite product lines across Tops/Tees, Shorts, and Pants in sizes S, M, L, and XL. Items purchased online may be mailed back or returned at a Zava flagship store when the store can process the tender type. Store purchases from `ST-AUS`, `ST-MIA`, or `ST-CHI` may be returned in store or by customer-support authorization.

## Worn or opened apparel
Opening the shipping bag or product polybag does not automatically prevent a return. The item must still meet the unworn, unwashed, tags-attached standard. Apparel that has been worn for workout, field, travel, or daily use is not returnable for buyer's remorse.

For hygiene and customer-safety reasons, final-sale hygiene-sensitive items cannot be returned once opened or tried on. If a customer reports a defect on worn or opened apparel, Zava reviews the case individually. Defect examples include seam failure, fabric flaw, incorrect size label, color-transfer issue before washing, or a manufacturing defect discovered during first reasonable use. Photos, order number, care steps, and the affected SKU may be required before a refund, replacement, or store credit decision is made.

## Free size exchanges
Zava offers free size exchanges within 60 days for eligible unworn, unwashed apparel with tags attached. Exchanges are intended for the same SKU family when only the size changes, such as moving from Medium to Large in the same product line, garment, gender, cut, and colorway. If the requested size is unavailable, customer support may offer a refund, store credit, or a comparable colorway with customer approval.

Exchange shipments are sent by standard service. Expedited exchange shipping may be offered when Zava shipped the wrong item or when a verified defect caused the exchange.

## Defects, wrong items, and final sale
If Zava shipped the wrong SKU, wrong size, wrong colorway, or a visibly damaged item, the return shipping fee is waived and no restocking fee applies. Clearance or final-sale items are not returnable for fit or preference unless defective or incorrectly shipped. Promotional bundles must be returned with all included items unless customer support authorizes a partial resolution.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are issued to the original payment method when possible. In-store returns are typically submitted the same day after inspection. Mailed returns are processed within 5 business days after arrival at the return facility. Payment providers may take an additional 3 to 7 business days to post funds. Store credit may be issued faster when the customer chooses it.
